# N+1 Labs

**Iterate hardware in simulation, not on the line.**

Today a hardware team finds out what breaks only after tooling, fixtures and the line are committed. Every late failure costs weeks. The lessons end up in slide decks and scattered databases and drives, so the next program learns them again.

N+1 Labs lets hardware teams find what fails in their next build before they build it. We push thousands of virtual units through a model of the line and show what will fail, how often, and which tolerance or station causes it. Change the design or the process, rerun it the same day, and see what moved.

## Three generations of every product

We see every product the way hardware teams number generations.

- **N−1 · Shipped.** The last generation, rebuilt with its factory from units bought at retail and published as Open Product.
- **N · Yours.** The line you run today. Your data stays on your site and never gets published.
- **N+1 · Next.** Your next build, run thousands of times in simulation before tooling is cut.

## Open Product

GitHub opened up code. Hugging Face opened up models. Open Product does that for hardware.

Each release is an OPD, an Open Product Description. It holds a shipped product's parts, bill of materials, suppliers, line and known failures, built on OpenUSD and STEP, so an agent can rebuild the product and its line. The first release is a game controller.
